# Business Diagnostic

Most founders try to fix everything at once, or pour effort (and ad spend) into the wrong place. A business that
isn't growing has **exactly one** reason — one part of the machine is the constraint, and improving any other part
is wasted because the system clogs at the constraint anyway. This skill finds that ONE constraint and names the
single thing to fix first. It stacks three diagnostics from the AI Business Blueprint: **TSS** (which of Traffic /
Systems / Skills is the bottleneck), **1-1-1** (are you scattered across too many variants?), and the
**Bottleneck-Finder** (assume the obvious problem is solved — does the rest keep up?). It is grounded by one law:
**Plumbing before Water** — never push more traffic through a system that doesn't hold.

## When to use
- The user asks "where is my business stuck", "what should I fix first", "why am I not growing", "should I scale".
- **Onboarding** an operator into EVE, or the operator onboarding a new client — establish the real constraint before building anything.
- **Before** any decision to spend on ads, add a traffic source, hire, or scale — run this as the pre-flight (it is the gate `landing-copy` and ad spend sit behind).
- Whenever the user is busy improving something that isn't the constraint (the most common failure: optimizing Traffic when Systems is broken).

## The method
Run all three lenses, then converge on ONE priority. Do NOT hand back a menu — the value is the single call.

1. **Frame the business honestly.** Capture what it is (service / agency / coach / SaaS), the current revenue reality,
   and where the operator *thinks* the problem is. Their self-diagnosis is a hypothesis to test, not the answer.

2. **TSS audit — score the three components.** Every service business is three things. Score each 1–10 and mark exactly ONE as the bottleneck:
   - **Traffic** (DE: *Traffic/Leads*) — "Do you have a reliable, near-unlimited source of qualified leads?" Job: fill the funnel.
   - **Systems** (DE: *Systeme*) — "Do leads progress to bookings / contracts / cash automatically, without you manually pulling each one?" Job: convert.
   - **Skills** (DE: *Skills/Lieferung*) — "Who fulfills? Is the skill transferable, or does everything depend on the founder?" Job: deliver and get paid.
   It is never all three at once — it is always one. The classic case is the **Skills bottleneck**: traffic ok, systems ok, but *the founder IS the delivery* → no scaling until that skill is transferred (SOPs → train a junior → co-delivery → phase out).

3. **1-1-1 check — are you scattered?** Count active **traffic sources × conversion mechanisms × delivery channels**.
   A clogged funnel across 5 channels isn't 5× harder to diagnose — it's 25× harder. If there's >1 in any area
   without a clear top performer *before the 1-1-1 is dialed* → the priority is **consolidate, don't add**: pause the
   weaker variants (don't delete), and dial the single best avatar / channel / offer first.

4. **Bottleneck-Finder — assume success.** Take the operator's presumed fix and dramatically *assume it's solved*
   ("we now have 10× the leads" / "we double revenue next month"). Then walk the chain: can we convert them? deliver?
   do we have the people, cashflow, systems? **Wherever it breaks is the real bottleneck** — and it must be fixed
   *before* the growth, not after. Run the standard four stress-assumptions: revenue ×2 · customers ×5 · operator
   away 4 weeks · a key person leaves.

5. **Apply Plumbing-before-Water + converge to ONE priority.** If Systems is weak, the constraint is Systems —
   period; no traffic spend until the pre-flight passes (lead-handling defined · clear conversion step · conversion
   rate known · onboarding documented · delivery reproducible · capacity counted). Then state the **single
   constraint**, why it dominates the others, and the **smallest viable fix** that holds the next 3 months (not the
   perfect system). One constraint. One next action. 30–90 days on it, then re-audit.

## Output
- The **TSS scorecard** (three scores 1–10, with the ONE bottleneck circled) and the **1-1-1 count** (scattered or dialed).
- The **Bottleneck-Finder result** — what breaks first when success is assumed, and whether the operator's self-diagnosis was right.
- The **Plumbing-before-Water verdict** — is it safe to add traffic/spend yet, or is the system not ready?
- **THE ONE PRIORITY**: the single constraint to fix first, *why it dominates*, and the **smallest viable fix** with a 30–90 day horizon — phrased as one decision, not a list.

## Key discipline
- **Exactly one bottleneck.** If the output names two, it isn't finished. Force the convergence.
- **Test the self-diagnosis.** Operators almost always name the wrong constraint ("we need more leads") — the
  Bottleneck-Finder exists to catch this. Surface the gap between what they *think* and what actually breaks.
- **Never green-light spend over weak plumbing.** Buying traffic before the system holds is the most expensive
  lesson there is — paid at full price. This is the hard gate.
- **Smallest viable, not perfect.** The fix only needs to hold the next 3 months, then re-assess.

## For Command EVE
This is a native EVE capability — the **first thing EVE runs with a new operator**, and the lens EVE returns to before
any consequential growth move (see `eve-doctrine`: pre-mortem and validate before the operator bets).

- **EVE diagnoses WITH the operator.** On onboarding, EVE walks the operator (Alois) through TSS + 1-1-1 +
  Bottleneck-Finder on *his own* business, names the one constraint, and hands it straight to `plan-system` — **the
  priority becomes the v1 milestone**, with child Worker Contracts hanging under it. The diagnostic isn't a one-off
  report; it re-runs each version cycle as the new bottleneck appears.
- **Then Alois diagnoses each client.** When the operator takes on a client, EVE runs the same diagnostic on the
  *client's* business first — so the work plan targets the client's real constraint, not a guessed one (invisible
  delivery: the client never sees EVE; see `eve-doctrine`).
- **It composes with the other skills.** The diagnostic decides *which* skill to deploy next: a **Traffic** bottleneck
  routes to `deep-research` (ICP/market) → `landing-copy`; a **Systems/scattered** bottleneck routes to `plan-system`
  to consolidate; an unproven offer routes to `icp-persona-panel`; and the final single call is packaged with
  `decision-brief` so the operator acts on ONE priority, not a wall of options.
- **Evidence rule.** Scores and the named constraint are the operator's honest input, not EVE's invention — any score
  EVE can't ground in what the operator actually reported is flagged as an assumption to confirm before betting on it.
