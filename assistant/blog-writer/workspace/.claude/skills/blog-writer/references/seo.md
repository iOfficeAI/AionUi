# SEO Reference Guide

## Keyword Strategy

### Primary Keyword
- Appears in: title, first paragraph, one H2, meta description, URL slug
- Frequency: 3-5 natural occurrences in a 2,000-word article
- Placement should feel organic — never forced or repeated unnaturally

### Secondary Keywords
- 3-5 related phrases that support the primary keyword
- Distributed across different sections
- Include long-tail variations and semantic synonyms

### Search Intent Alignment

| Intent | Signal | Article Approach |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Informational | "what is", "how to", "guide" | Educational, comprehensive, structured |
| Commercial | "best", "vs", "review", "comparison" | Evaluative, data-driven, decision-supporting |
| Navigational | "[brand] + feature" | Specific, direct, solution-focused |

## On-Page SEO Checklist

### Title Tag
- Include primary keyword, preferably near the beginning
- 50-60 characters optimal length
- Make it compelling — SEO and click-through rate are both goals

### Meta Description
- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword naturally
- Create curiosity or promise value — this is your search result pitch

### URL Slug
- Short: 3-5 words
- Hyphen-separated, lowercase
- Include primary keyword
- No dates, numbers that will become outdated, or filler words

### Heading Structure
- H1: Article title (one per page)
- H2: Main sections (include primary or secondary keyword in 1-2)
- H3: Subsections
- Logical hierarchy — never skip levels

### Internal/External Links
- Link to 2-3 relevant internal pages if available
- Link to 3-5 authoritative external sources (research, original data)
- Descriptive anchor text — not "click here"

## Content Freshness Signals

- Include current year in title/content where relevant
- Reference recent data (within 2 years)
- Mention current tools, frameworks, or trends
- Date the article explicitly in metadata

## Structured Data Hints

Include metadata that enables rich snippets:
- FAQ sections (question + answer format)
- How-to steps (numbered sequential instructions)
- Author attribution
- Publication date
- Article type (BlogPosting, TechArticle, HowTo)

## Common SEO Mistakes

- **Keyword stuffing** — repeating the keyword 15 times in 500 words
- **Thin content** — articles under 800 words rarely rank for competitive terms
- **Missing alt text** — every image needs a descriptive alt attribute
- **Orphan pages** — articles with no internal links to or from them
- **Duplicate intent** — two articles targeting the same keyword cannibalize each other
