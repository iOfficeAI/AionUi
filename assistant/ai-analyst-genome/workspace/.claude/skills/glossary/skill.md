## Common Terms

`/glossary`

```
Vietnamese Stock Market Glossary
==================================

EXCHANGES
  HOSE    Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (So Giao dich Chung khoan TP.HCM)
  HNX     Hanoi Stock Exchange (So Giao dich Chung khoan Ha Noi)
  UPCOM   Unlisted Public Company Market

INDICES
  VN-Index   HOSE composite index
  VN30       Top 30 stocks by market cap and liquidity on HOSE
  HNX-Index  HNX composite index

INSTITUTIONS
  SSC     State Securities Commission (Uy ban Chung khoan Nha nuoc)
  SBV     State Bank of Vietnam (Ngan hang Nha nuoc Viet Nam)
  VSD     Vietnam Securities Depository (Trung tam Luu ky Chung khoan)

TRADING
  Tran     Price ceiling (gia tran) - upper daily limit
  San      Price floor (gia san) - lower daily limit
  TC       Reference price (gia tham chieu) - previous close
  T+2      Settlement cycle (2 business days after trade)
  ATO      At-the-open order (lenh ATO)
  ATC      At-the-close order (lenh ATC)

METRICS
  P/E      He so gia tren thu nhap (Price-to-Earnings)
  P/B      He so gia tren gia tri so sach (Price-to-Book)
  ROE      Ty suat sinh loi tren von chu so huu (Return on Equity)
  ROA      Ty suat sinh loi tren tong tai san (Return on Assets)
  EPS      Thu nhap tren co phieu (Earnings per Share)

Type /glossary [term] for detailed definition.
```
