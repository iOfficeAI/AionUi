## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The macro regime as of February 2026 is characterized by: (1) Fed easing cycle in progress (fed funds at 4.00%, 2 cuts delivered in 2025), (2) resilient growth (GDP +2.4% annualized), (3) services inflation remaining sticky at 3.8% PCE, and (4) a secular AI infrastructure capex cycle that is acting as a countercyclical growth driver. The primary investment theme: AI compute infrastructure is the dominant capex allocation for technology sector — creating a structural tailwind for semiconductors, data center REITs, and power infrastructure. Equities are preferred over fixed income; US mega-cap tech and infrastructure are the highest-conviction overweights.

The AI infrastructure supercycle is adding an exogenous demand layer to the economy that is partly insulating technology capex from cyclical headwinds. This regime favors: (1) large-cap quality growth equities, (2) AI infrastructure plays, (3) investment-grade credit, and (4) power/utility infrastructure linked to data center demand.

AI growth stocks — which have long duration cash flows — are direct beneficiaries.

## INFLATION & GROWTH

**Source**: Web research — BLS, BEA, Federal Reserve data

**CPI / Core CPI**: 3.1% YoY / 3.4% YoY (January 2026)
**PCE / Core PCE**: 2.6% / 2.8% (December 2025 — Fed's preferred measure)
**GDP Growth**: +2.4% annualized (Q4 2025 advance estimate), +2.7% consensus next 4 quarters
**Unemployment**: 4.2% | Nonfarm Payrolls: +182K (January 2026)

**Inflation Trajectory**: Services inflation (4.1% YoY) remains elevated — driven by shelter (+5.2%), healthcare (+3.8%), and transportation (+6.1%). Goods deflation (-0.8%) is providing offset. The Fed expects Core PCE to reach 2.3% by end-2026 — the path requires continued services disinflation without demand destruction.

**AI's Inflationary Impact**: The AI compute buildout is contributing to a microinflationary dynamic in specific commodities: electricity (+8.2% in data center markets), high-bandwidth memory (+23% pricing), and specialized labor (AI engineers' salaries +31% YoY). These are idiosyncratic, not broad-based.

**Growth Risk**: Recession probability (12-month) at 18% (NY Fed model) — below historical risk levels. Leading indicators: yield curve slightly inverted (-12bps 10Y-2Y), ISM Manufacturing 49.8 (marginally contractionary), ISM Services 53.2 (expansionary). Soft landing base case remains most probable.

## AI INFRASTRUCTURE MACRO THEME

**This section specific to AI/semiconductor macro focus of this engagement**

**Source**: Web research + training knowledge

**Capex Cycle Magnitude**: The four major hyperscalers (AMZN, MSFT, GOOGL, META) announced combined 2025 AI-related capex of ~$300B — up 72% from 2024's ~$175B. 2026 guidance implies continued growth to ~$380B.

**Demand Drivers**:

1. **Training demand**: Large model training (GPT-6, Gemini Ultra 2, Claude 5) requires exascale compute clusters — demand is insatiable at current model scaling laws
2. **Inference demand**: AI APIs processing billions of requests/day — inference becoming the larger revenue driver for NVDA vs training
3. **Sovereign AI**: Governments of Saudi Arabia, UAE, France, Japan, India, Singapore allocating $3-15B each for national AI compute infrastructure — a newly unlocked demand vector
4. **Enterprise AI**: SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Oracle embedding AI — enterprise hardware refresh cycle driving edge compute demand

**Supply Constraints**: TSMC 3nm/2nm capacity remains the binding constraint — NVDA, AMD, Apple, AAPL competing for same foundry capacity. CoWoS advanced packaging (HBM3e stacking) is a secondary bottleneck. Both are easing through 2026 as TSMC expands Arizona fabs.

## GEOPOLITICAL & STRUCTURAL RISKS

**Top 3 Macro Risks** (Source: web research):

1. **US-China AI Technology War** — Probability: Medium-High | Market impact: Severe (for semis)
   - Escalating export controls on AI chips could close the $30B+ China AI semiconductor market to US companies. China is accelerating domestic chip development (Huawei, SMIC) — the technology gap is narrowing.

2. **AI Bubble / Capex Overkill** — Probability: Low-Medium | Market impact: Severe (for AI stocks)
   - If enterprise AI ROI proves disappointing, hyperscaler capex could reset 30-40% lower in 2027. NVDA forward P/E would compress from 34x to 20x on lower revenue trajectory — stock could halve.

3. **Geopolitical Conflict Escalation (Taiwan)** — Probability: Low | Market impact: Catastrophic
   - Taiwan Strait military conflict would interrupt TSMC production (54% of global advanced node capacity) — all AI chipmakers affected. Semiconductor supply shock would be deflationary for AI buildout.
