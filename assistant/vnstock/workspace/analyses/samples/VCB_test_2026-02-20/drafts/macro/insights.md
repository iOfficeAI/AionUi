# Macroeconomic Analysis

## Regime Classification

**Current Regime**: EXPANSION
**Confidence**: 100%
**Date**: 2026-02-20

## Key Indicators

- **GDP Growth**: 7.2% (YoY)
- **Credit Growth**: 14.5% (YoY)
- **Inflation (CPI)**: 4.2% (YoY)

## Interpretation

Vietnam's economy is in a robust expansion phase. Strong GDP growth above 7%, healthy credit expansion at 14.5%, and moderate inflation at 4.2% all point to economic momentum without overheating. This is an ideal scenario for risk assets, particularly cyclical sectors.

The combination of strong growth and contained inflation suggests monetary policy will remain accommodative, supporting further equity market gains.

## Sector Implications

**Favored Sectors**: Banks, Real Estate, Industrials, Consumer Discretionary

- **BANKS** - Loan growth accelerating, NIM expansion from credit demand
- **REAL_ESTATE** - Infrastructure spending, urbanization tailwinds
- **INDUSTRIALS** - Manufacturing export growth, FDI inflows
- **CONSUMER_DISCRETIONARY** - Rising incomes, consumption upgrade

**Sectors to Avoid**: Defensive sectors (utilities, consumer staples) likely to underperform

## Factor Implications

**Favored Factors**: Momentum, Growth, Beta

- **MOMENTUM** - Trend-following works in expansions
- **GROWTH** - High-growth companies benefit from economic tailwinds
- **BETA** - High-beta stocks outperform in risk-on environments

## Risk Flags

- If inflation crosses 5.5%, State Bank may tighten policy, transitioning to SLOWDOWN
- External risks: Fed policy, China slowdown could impact exports
- Credit quality: Rapid loan growth may mask deteriorating underwriting

## Bottom Line

Vietnam is in mid-cycle expansion with favorable conditions for cyclical equities. Banks are best positioned given credit demand and margin expansion. Recommend overweight BANKS, REAL_ESTATE, and high-momentum growth stocks.
