# Fundamental Analysis: VCB

## Financial Health Score

**Overall**: STRONG
**Date**: 2026-02-20

## Profitability

- **ROE**: 22.5% (return on equity)
- **ROA**: 1.2% (return on assets)
- **ROIC**: 18.0% (return on invested capital)
- **Net Interest Margin**: 3.8%
- **Cost/Income Ratio**: 35%

**Assessment**: VCB demonstrates excellent profitability metrics. ROE of 22.5% is top-quartile for Vietnamese banks and significantly above the sector average of 16%. Efficient operations (35% cost/income) and strong NIM (3.8%) indicate pricing power and operational leverage.

## Growth

- **Loan Book CAGR (3Y)**: 15.0%
- **Net Interest Income CAGR (3Y)**: 17.0%
- **Net Profit CAGR (3Y)**: 18.0%

**Assessment**: Growth is robust and above industry average. Loan book expansion at 15% CAGR is driven by organic customer acquisition and market share gains. Profit growth outpacing loan growth indicates positive operating leverage and margin expansion.

## Balance Sheet

- **NPL Ratio**: 0.8%
- **Loan Loss Coverage**: 180%
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR)**: 12.5%
- **Liquidity Coverage Ratio**: 150%

**Assessment**: Best-in-class balance sheet strength. NPL ratio of 0.8% is the lowest among major Vietnamese banks, reflecting conservative underwriting. CAR well above regulatory minimum (9%) provides buffer for growth. Excellent liquidity position.

## Competitive Advantages (Moats)

- **State ownership** - 75% government-owned, implicit sovereign guarantee
- **Branch network** - 1,000+ branches, most extensive in Vietnam
- **Brand strength** - #1 brand in banking sector, premium customer segment
- **Digital leadership** - Leading mobile banking app, 10M+ active users

## Red Flags

None significant. Minor concern: Deposit growth slowing in Q4 2025 (12% YoY vs 15% in Q3), may compress NIM if trend continues.

## Bottom Line

**BUY** - VCB exhibits exceptional fundamental strength with top-tier profitability, pristine asset quality, and sustainable growth. State ownership provides stability and funding advantage. Current valuation (P/B 2.5x) is reasonable given superior ROE and market position. Target price 110,000 VND (+12% upside).
